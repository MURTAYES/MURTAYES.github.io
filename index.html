Clinical Dispensing POS & FEFO Batch Accounting Kernel

A clinical pharmacy dispensing terminal and high-speed POS kernel engineered for retail counters. Features batch-wise FEFO inventory tracking, multi-unit conversions (Piece ↔ Strip ↔ Box), and Decimal128 ledger precision.

Why This Project

To eliminate retail counter bottlenecks, prevent expired medicine dispensing loss, and maintain immutable financial ledgers at microsecond transaction speeds.

Core Technical Highlights
  • Automated FEFO Engine: Dispenses nearest-expiry inventory first with sub-300ms counter latency.
  • Multi-Unit Conversion: Seamless bidirectional conversion across wholesale cartons, blister strips, and unit tablets.
  • Decimal128 Ledger Precision: Zero float-rounding errors across dual-entry accounting journals.
