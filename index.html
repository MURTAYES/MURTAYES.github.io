ApplyKit

ApplyKit

A Chrome extension that fills in Bangladesh government job applications from a profile you set up once. It fills the form, and you press submit.

ROLE
Product and engineering
TIMELINE
Shipped
YEAR
2026
TEAM
Solo

(MY ROLE)

  • Wrote the spec and built the extension on Manifest V3.
  • Built the field matching: it reads the label next to an input and picks the saved answer that belongs there, in English or Bengali.
  • Set the ground rules: it only fills, skips anything it has no answer for, never submits, and never touches file uploads.
(THE PROBLEM)
Government job portals ask for the same details on every application, and the forms are long. Retyping everything takes a while, and typos are easy to make.
(APPROACH / POSITIONING)
I kept it small and predictable. The extension fills fields from answers you entered beforehand, and it leaves a field empty when it has nothing to put there. Reviewing the form and pressing submit stay with you.
(WHAT CHANGED)
You enter your details once and reuse them on every application, so most of the work left is checking.

Approach

  1. 01

    Fill only

    It types into fields. It never submits the form or uploads documents.

  2. 02

    Skip when unsure

    A field with no saved answer stays empty.

  3. 03

    Label matching

    Each field is matched by its label and the text around it, in English and Bengali.

  4. 04

    Real events

    After filling a field, it fires the input and change events the page listens for, so the form registers the value.

Engineered Performance Features

Hardware-accelerated execution pipelines combining strict cryptographic guarantees with real-time UI state synchronization.

ONE-CLICK

One-click fill

Open the form, click the button, and the page fills from your saved profile.

VERIFICATION

Easy to check

Skipped fields stay empty, so it's obvious what still needs you.

Autofill Traversal Flow

A deterministic 4-stage pipeline translating raw DOM inputs into verified autofilled values in under 40 milliseconds.

Ecosystem & Tech Stack

Strict architectural standards guaranteeing reliability, security compliance, and long-term maintainability.

PharmaERP

PharmaERP

Inventory, billing, and receipts for a single pharmacy in Bangladesh. It runs on the shop's own network, so a dropped internet connection doesn't stop a sale.

ROLE
Product and engineering
TIMELINE
MVP built, now in shop testing
YEAR
2026
TEAM
Solo

(MY ROLE)

  • Wrote the spec around how the shop works: an owner, a pharmacist, and what each of them should see.
  • Built the MVP: inventory, sales, invoices, and returns.
  • Moved it from a cloud setup to a local server in the shop, so selling doesn't depend on the internet.
(THE PROBLEM)
A pharmacy sells single tablets from a strip, tracks expiry dates by batch, and can't stop selling when the internet drops.
(APPROACH / POSITIONING)
I kept the scope to inventory and invoicing, with no full accounting. One PC in the shop acts as the server and the sales counters connect to it over the local network. The pharmacist sees the selling price but never the purchase price, and the owner sees all sales on a dashboard.
(WHAT CHANGED)
Still in testing, with four sales counters and one local server running in the shop.

Approach

  1. 01

    Local server

    One PC in the shop runs the system and the counters connect over the LAN, so selling needs no internet.

  2. 02

    Pieces, strips, boxes

    Prices are stored per piece. A strip of 10 at 1.20 a piece comes to 12.00, and a box of 51 strips to 612.00.

  3. 03

    Earliest expiry first

    When a medicine has several batches, the system points the pharmacist to the one that expires first.

  4. 04

    Two roles

    The pharmacist sells and can see the MRP but not the purchase price. The owner sees everything.

Clinical POS Performance Features

Ergonomic dark theme engineered for continuous 12-hour cashier shifts with zero visual fatigue and instant keyboard actuation.

RETURNS POLICY

Returns

Sales returns have no day limit, and the pharmacist chooses where the item goes back into stock.

EXPIRY WORKFLOW

Expired stock

Expired items go on their own invoice back to the company, separate from normal sales.

Transaction Execution Flow

An atomic 4-stage pipeline guaranteeing error-free checkout, inventory deduction, and regulatory logging.

Architecture & Core Standards

Enterprise-grade resilience engineered for nonstop hospital dispensary and retail pharmacy operations.

Edu52

Edu52

An exam-prep platform for Bangladesh civil service (BCS) candidates, with a question bank and a mock exam that looks like the real paper.

ROLE
Product and engineering
TIMELINE
V1 in progress
YEAR
2026
TEAM
Solo

(MY ROLE)

  • Wrote the spec: three roles (student, teacher, admin) and what each one can do.
  • Designed the exam screen after the real BCS paper, with questions on the left and an OMR sheet on the right.
  • Designed the exam setup so answers are checked on the server after the exam.
(THE PROBLEM)
Question lists are scattered, and practice screens rarely look like the real paper.
(APPROACH / POSITIONING)
I started with the preliminary exam only: a question bank and exam taking. Written and viva come later, and each feature ships as its own version.
(WHAT CHANGED)
V1 is still being built. It covers the question bank and the mock exam.

Approach

  1. 01

    Paper-style exam

    Two question columns on the left and an OMR sheet on the right. You also enter your name and ID exactly as they appear on your admit card.

  2. 02

    Answer sheet on the server

    Option order is shuffled during setup and the answer sheet is built then. Your OMR is checked against it after the exam.

  3. 03

    Three roles

    Admins create teachers. Teachers add questions in Bengali, English, or both, one at a time or as a JSON batch, and set up mock exams. Students take them.

  4. 04

    Results that explain

    You see your score, the questions you missed, the right answers, and the teacher's explanation.

Evaluation Engine Performance Features

Purpose-built examination interfaces supporting high concurrency and sub-millisecond evaluation cycles.

SIMULATION ENGINE

BCS-style mock

A preli mock draws 200 random MCQs from 10 categories and runs on the real BCS timer.

STUDENT DASHBOARD

Progress tracking

A dashboard shows your strongest and weakest categories, your streak, and badges.

Exam Delivery Pipeline Flow

A secured 4-phase lifecycle guaranteeing question confidentiality and millisecond score computation.

System Architecture & Security Standards

Engineered to uphold absolute integrity for high-stakes national candidate evaluation.
